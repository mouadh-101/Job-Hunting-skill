# Cross-Regional Recruitment & ATS Adaptation Matrix

Hiring cultures and Applicant Tracking Systems vary significantly across global regions. **Job-Hunting-skill** tailors documents according to regional market realities.

---

## Regional Comparison Matrix

| Dimension | European Union (EU / UK) | United States & Canada (US/CA) | Gulf & UAE (GCC) | Nearshore & Emerging Tech Hubs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dominant ATS Suites** | Personio, Workable, Recruitee, Workday, Greenhouse | Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, Taleo | Bayt.com (Talentera), Bayzat, Qureos, LinkedIn | Keejob, Welcome to the Jungle, Lever, Taleo |
| **Parsing Technologies** | Textkernel (multilingual parsing), LLM vector rankers | Sophisticated Boolean filters + contextual LLM embeddings | Bayt AI+ sourcing assistant, neural resume indexers | Local portal filters + European corporate parent ATS |
| **Key Compliance & Laws** | Strict GDPR compliance; no photos, no age, no marital status | Strict EEOC/labor laws; zero personal photos or demographic data | Requires explicit residency/visa status, nationality, availability | Nearshore frameworks (e.g. CIVP/SIVP in Tunisia, nearshore contracts) |
| **Primary Knock-Out Criteria** | Work authorization / Visa sponsorship, degree accreditation | US work authorization (Citizen / Green Card / OPT / H1-B) | In-country vs overseas applicant, immediate notice period | Contract eligibility, French/English bilingualism, tech stack fit |
| **Document Aesthetic & Length** | Strict 1-page (for <7 years exp), clean single-column | Strict 1-page, high numerical density, zero graphics | 1 or 2 pages, explicit visa status & notice period stated | 1-page, dual French/English technical terminology |

## Tailoring Strategies per Region

### 1. European Union (EU) Applications
- **Anonymity & Merit:** Keep the layout strictly text-focused without photos or personal demographics.
- **Language:** English is standard for international tech hubs (Berlin, Amsterdam, Dublin, Stockholm). For domestic companies in France, Germany, or Switzerland, use the native language (`templates/cv_template_fr.tex`).
- **EUR-ACE / Accreditation:** For engineering degrees, highlight accredited titles (e.g. *EUR-ACE® Master-level accreditation*).

### 2. North America (USA & Canada)
- **Aggressive ASO Metrics:** US hiring managers demand hard metrics ($ revenue, % efficiency, user count).
- **Work Authorization:** If applying without requiring sponsorship, explicitly state *"US Citizen / Green Card / Authorized to work in the US without sponsorship"* in the header.

### 3. United Arab Emirates & Gulf (GCC)
- **Immediate Availability & Visa:** Sourcing algorithms on Bayt and LinkedIn prioritize candidates with *Immediate Availability* or short notice periods.
- **Explicit Location:** Clearly state your current location and openness to relocation in Dubai, Riyadh, Abu Dhabi, or Doha.

### 4. Nearshore Hubs (Tunisia, Morocco, Eastern Europe)
- **Contract Schemes:** Mention state employment incentives (such as *CIVP / SIVP eligibility* in Tunisia).
- **Dual Language Fluency:** Highlight professional bilingualism (e.g. *French fluent / English C1 professional*).
