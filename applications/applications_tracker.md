### Status Legend
- 📝 `Drafted` — Documents compiled and ready to submit
- 🚀 `Applied` — Submitted via portal or email
- 💬 `In Outreach` — Direct LinkedIn / email pitch sent to decision maker
- 📞 `Phone Screen` — Initial recruiter interview scheduled
- 🧑‍💻 `Technical Interview` — Coding test, case study, or technical interview
- 🎉 `Offer Received` — Formal employment offer made
- 🏁 `Closed` — Position closed or declined
